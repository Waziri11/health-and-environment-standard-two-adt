Read the following case study and answer the questions that follow:

Sopwe and Kipepe are standard two pupils at Mtakuja Primary School. The school is located along a main road that has a children’s crossing sign. One day, Sopwe and Kipepe went to buy sweets from a shop located across the road. They crossed a section of the road that did not have a children’s crossing sign. As they crossed, they were struck by a motorcycle. Sopwe broke his arm, and Kipepe sustained bruises on his knees. Their classmates called a teacher, who took them to the hospital for treatment. The teacher instructed them always use the children’s crossing whenever they cross the road. Additionally, they should ensure that bicycles, motorcycles, and cars have stopped before crossing.

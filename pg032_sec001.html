Activity 3

Design and develop an item that can be used for cleaning the environment and explain its use.

The environment is cleaned using different items such as a hoe, broom, cloth, rake, dustpan, watering can, slasher, garden shears, soap, dustbin, machete, and bucket. These items can be bought or made using materials available in our surroundings

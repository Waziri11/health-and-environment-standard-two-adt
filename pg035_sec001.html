Chausiku explained, as the doctor had told her, that “Malaria is caused by parasites that are transmitted to human through mosquitoes. Mosquitoes breed in environments with stagnant water, long grass, and in containers and tires that hold water. To prevent malaria, it is necessary to remove mosquito breeding sites by cutting long grasses and removing stagnant water. Additionally, containers and tires that hold water should be removed from our environment. Moreover, cleaning the environment keeps away other dangerous animals like snakes, monitor lizards, scorpions, and centipedes. The pupils were happy and promised to clean their environment.

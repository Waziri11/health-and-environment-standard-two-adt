Health and Environment Pupil’s Book Standard Two

Activity 4

Perform a role-play to demonstrate adherence to safety signs in the environment.

There are several safety signs in our environment. These signs are used to direct, restrict, warn, and indicate. They also show possible dangers that can occur if they are not observed. Examples of safety signs include no open flame, no smoking, unsafe water, slippery floor, dump waste here, flammable, high voltage, and toxic materials.

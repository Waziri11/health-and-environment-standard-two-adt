Poem

Sing the following poem, then answer the question.

Poem
When walking, safety is a priority,
When on the road, know the road signs,
Home or school, arrive safely,
Safety is a priority when on the road.
Walking on the right, it is always safe,
On the pedestrian crossing, look to the right,
To the left, then to the right again,
Safety is a priority, when on the road.
When cars stop, that’s when it is safe,
You can cross safely, without getting stuck,
The things you have read, keep in mind,
Safety is a priority, when on the road.

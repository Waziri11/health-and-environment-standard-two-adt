Health and Environment Pupil’s Book Standard Two

Activity 5

Use the items used for cleaning the environment to clean the following areas:

(i)Your classroom

(ii)The school toilet

(iii)The school compound

A clean environment is attractive and provides us with fresh air.

It also keeps away dangerous animals and insects that spread diseases.

We should clean our home and school environments to keep them clean and safe.

Also, we should wash our hands with clean water and soap after cleaning the environment to protect ourselves from diseases.
